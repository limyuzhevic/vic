
## System Status Summary

Phase 6 represents a major leap from the basic neural simulation of Phase 2 to a coherent artificial brain. The architecture follows a closed-loop design where neural activity, plasticity, memory, neuromodulation, and behavior continuously influence each other.

### Cognitive Capabilities:
- **Working Memory**: Active maintenance of sensory patterns
- **Episodic Memory**: Storage and replay of experiences
- **Prediction System**: Expectation formation and error computation
- **Neural Planner**: Action sequence evaluation
- **Concept Formation**: Pattern categorization and generalization

### Neuromodulatory Functions:
- **Dopamine**: Reward prediction error and learning modulation
- **Acetylcholine**: Attention and memory consolidation
- **Norepinephrine**: Arousal and behavioral flexibility
- **Serotonin**: Mood regulation and impulsivity control
- **Curiosity**: Exploration drive
- **Novelty**: Change detection

### System Integration:
- **Sensory Processing**: Complete pipeline from environment to neural representation
- **Action Selection**: Multiple factors influence motor command selection
- **Learning**: Synaptic plasticity modulated by multiple signals
- **Memory**: Active maintenance, encoding, consolidation, and replay
- **Development**: Stage-dependent changes in system parameters

The Phase 6 architecture transforms the NLM from a basic neural simulator into an integrated artificial brain capable of cognitive functions including perception, memory, learning, planning, and goal-directed behavior.
